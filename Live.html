        Check the bill

        Take a straight photo of the whole receipt in good light. The app finds items you did not scan and prices that are different.

        • Lay the bill flat on a dark table, with no shadow or shine on it.
        • Long bill? Take it in parts, from top to bottom. Start each photo two lines above where the last one ended.
        • Check the bill on the day you shop. The print fades in a few weeks.
